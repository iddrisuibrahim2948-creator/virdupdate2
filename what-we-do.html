PROGRAMME 09

Islamic Social Finance

Faith-Inspired Social Investment. VIRD seeks to mobilize Islamic social finance as a tool for sustainable community transformation.

Zakat

Supporting eligible beneficiaries in accordance with applicable principles and requirements.

Sadaqah

Voluntary charitable giving to support humanitarian and development needs.

Waqf

Developing sustainable charitable assets capable of generating long-term social benefit.

Qard Hasan

Supporting appropriate interest-free financing mechanisms for productive economic activities.

Sadaqah Jariyah

Supporting lasting charitable initiatives whose benefits continue over time.

Restricted Islamic funds will be appropriately tracked, managed and reported according to their purpose and applicable requirements.
