Strategic Plan 2027–2031

Transforming Lives Through Faith, Compassion and Sustainable Development

GOVERNED BY OUR PRINCIPLES

Amanah

Our institutional conduct is guided by Amanah (trustworthiness), Adl (justice), Shura (consultation), Ihsan (excellence) and Rahmah (mercy) — alongside good governance, accountability, transparency, quality, impact and integrity.

STRATEGIC PLAN OVERVIEW

Ten elements of our strategic direction

This page presents the strategic plan in a readable web format. The original strategic plan document remains the source of truth.

Strategic Theme

From Relief to Resilience, From Vulnerability to Opportunity.

Strategic Ambition

By 2031, to become a credible, accountable, community-centred organization with strong institutional systems, diversified funding and effective community partnerships. See "Ambition to 2031" below for the full statement.

Strategic Objectives

Twelve objectives spanning humanitarian action, essential services, food security, livelihoods, women and youth, protection, Islamic social finance, community institutions, environment, peacebuilding, institutional strength and partnerships. See "Strategic Objectives" below for the full list.

Strategic Approach

An integrated continuum connecting relief, recovery, resilience, empowerment and opportunity.

Strategic Pillars

Fourteen programme pillars spanning humanitarian assistance through research, innovation and knowledge.

Flagship Programme

VIRD BARAKA — the integrated Community Transformation Programme.

Cross-Cutting Themes

Gender equality and inclusion, disability inclusion, child protection, good governance, safeguarding and PSEA, environment and climate considerations.

Strategic Results

Improved access to services, stronger resilience, increased incomes, stronger protection systems, enhanced Islamic social investment, stronger institutional capacity, accountability and sustainability.

Implementation Framework

Governance, resourcing and monitoring arrangements that carry the plan from 2027 to 2031.

Our Commitment

Accountable, transparent and community-centred delivery throughout the strategic period.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

What VIRD will work to achieve

VIRD's 2027–2031 Strategic Plan is built around these twelve objectives.

1. Humanitarian action

Save lives, protect dignity and provide timely humanitarian assistance.

2. Essential services

Improve access to WASH, health, education and nutrition.

3. Food and climate resilience

Enhance food security, agricultural productivity and climate resilience.

4. Economic opportunity

Create sustainable livelihoods and promote economic empowerment.

5. Women and youth

Advance women and youth inclusion, leadership and opportunities.

6. Protection and wellbeing

Strengthen child protection, social protection and community well-being.

7. Islamic social finance

Promote Zakat, Sadaqah, Waqf and Qard Hasan.

8. Community institutions

Strengthen mosques, Islamic education, libraries and community institutions.

9. Environmental stewardship

Build environmental stewardship and climate resilience.

10. Peace and accountable governance

Foster peacebuilding, social cohesion and accountable governance.

11. Institutional strength

Build a strong, accountable, innovative and sustainable institution.

12. Partnerships and sustainability

Maximize partnerships, resource mobilization and financial sustainability.

AMBITION TO 2031

A credible, accountable, community-centred organization

By 2031, VIRD seeks strong institutional systems; diversified, sustainable funding; effective community partnerships; demonstrated programme impact; safeguarding and accountability systems; robust monitoring, evaluation and learning; a recognized BARAKA programme; a strong Northern Ghana presence; and sustainable partnerships with government, donors, Islamic institutions, private sector and communities.

DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Systems that strengthen delivery and accountability

VIRD will progressively develop digital systems for programme and institutional management. Proposed platforms include beneficiary management, project management, donor relationship and grant management, a MEAL dashboard, BARAKA sponsorship, digital financial tracking and a digital asset register.

Public website services

The website is intended to be responsive and mobile-first, initially in English, search-engine optimized, integrated with social media, and capable of supporting online donations, volunteer registration, partnership enquiries, newsletter subscription and contact management.

Future digital services

VIRD Donor Portal, BARAKA Sponsorship Portal, beneficiary/community database, project and impact dashboards, online Zakat calculator, Waqf management platform, volunteer management system, and a digital complaints and feedback system.
