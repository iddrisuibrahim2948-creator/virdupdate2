LEARN. SHARE. IMPROVE.

Resources VIRD will publish

The Knowledge Centre is the home for verified information from VIRD programmes and institutional work.

Documents and evidence

Programme briefs, thematic papers, publications, annual reports, project reports, impact reports, studies, assessments, research findings, strategic plans and institutional frameworks.

Project learning

Project briefs, case studies and knowledge products that explain programme outcomes when verified and appropriate to publish.

News and media

Updates from communities and programmes, including humanitarian response, BARAKA, community development, education, health, WASH, agriculture, women, youth, Islamic social finance, climate and environment, partnerships, events, research and learning. Photo and video galleries will be published with appropriate consent and safeguarding controls.
