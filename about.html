About VIRD Foundation

Vantageway Islamic Relief & Development Foundation — who we are, what we believe, and how we are governed.

WHO WE ARE

A trusted partner for humanitarian and development work in Ghana

Vantageway Islamic Relief & Development Foundation (VIRD Foundation) is a humanitarian and development organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable individuals and communities through integrated humanitarian assistance, sustainable development, Islamic social investment and community transformation

VIRD works to address the interconnected challenges that affect vulnerable households and communities—including poverty, food insecurity, inadequate access to water and sanitation, limited educational opportunities, health and nutrition challenges, unemployment, social exclusion, environmental vulnerability and inadequate community infrastructure.

Our approach is grounded in human dignity, compassion, accountability, inclusion, community ownership, Islamic ethics and sustainable development.

We believe that lasting change requires more than emergency assistance.

It requires helping people recover, become resilient, gain skills and assets, access opportunities and participate meaningfully in the development of their communities.

Our Vision

A resilient, empowered, and dignified society where vulnerable individuals and communities have equitable opportunities to thrive and achieve sustainable well-being

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable individuals and communities through faith-inspired humanitarian assistance, inclusive and sustainable development programmes, community resilience, socioeconomic empowerment and strategic partnerships, guided by accountability, compassion, dignity, innovation and responsible stewardship of charitable resources.

OUR STORY

Why VIRD Foundation Exists

VIRD Foundation was established to respond to a clear and continuing reality: across Northern Ghana, vulnerable individuals and communities face overlapping humanitarian, development and social challenges that require more than short-term assistance. VIRD Foundation exists to connect immediate relief with long-term, sustainable development — combining accountable humanitarian assistance, integrated development programming and Islamic social investment into a single, coherent approach captured in our strategic theme: From Relief to Resilience, From Vulnerability to Opportunity.

Our work is grounded in the belief that humanitarian assistance alone is not enough — that lasting change requires resilience-building, community ownership and Islamic ethics of compassion, generosity and shared responsibility working together.

OUR STRATEGIC APPROACH

From relief to resilience

VIRD's development philosophy follows a transformation pathway:

Our transformation pathway

Vulnerability → Relief → Recovery → Resilience → Empowerment → Opportunity → Dignity → Sustainable Development.

How we work

We connect immediate humanitarian assistance with longer-term development interventions.

Community-first practice

Before major interventions, we use community consultations, baseline assessments, household surveys, vulnerability analysis, stakeholder mapping, gender analysis, disability inclusion analysis, environmental assessment and community priority ranking.

Our approach is:

Community-centred, evidence-based, faith-inspired, inclusive, accountable, sustainable and partnership-driven.

STRATEGIC PLAN 2027–2031

Our Five Year Strategic Direction

The VIRD Foundation Strategic Plan 2027–2031 provides the organization’s institutional and programme roadmap for transforming vulnerable communities through integrated humanitarian and development interventions.

Strategic Theme

From Relief to Resilience, From Vulnerability to Opportunity.

Strategic Ambition

By 2031, VIRD Foundation seeks to establish itself as a credible, accountable and community-centred humanitarian and development organization with:

  1. Strong institutional systems
  2. Diversified and sustainable funding
  3. Effective community partnerships
  4. Demonstrated programme impact
  5. Strong safeguarding and accountability systems
  6. Robust monitoring, evaluation and learning
  7. A recognized flagship BARAKA Community Transformation Programme
  8. Strong presence in Northern Ghana
  9. Sustainable partnerships with government, donors, Islamic institutions, private sector and communities

Strategic Objectives

VIRD's Strategic Plan 2027–2031 is built around the following objectives:

  1. Save lives, protect dignity and provide timely humanitarian assistance.
  2. Improve access to essential services including WASH, health, education and nutrition.
  3. Enhance food security, agricultural productivity and climate resilience.
  4. Create sustainable livelihoods and promote economic empowerment.
  5. Advance women and youth inclusion, leadership and opportunities.
  6. Strengthen child protection, social protection and community well-being.
  7. Promote Islamic social finance including Zakat, Sadaqah, Waqf and Qard Hasan.
  8. Strengthen mosques, Islamic education, libraries and community institutions.
  9. Build environmental stewardship and climate resilience.
  10. Foster peacebuilding, social cohesion and accountable governance.
  11. Build a strong, accountable, innovative and sustainable institution.
  12. Maximize partnerships, resource mobilization and financial sustainability.

Cross-Cutting Themes

VIRD foundation aids by:

  1. Social Behavior Change Communication (SBCC)
  2. Gender Equality, Social Inclusion and Safeguarding
  3. Climate-Induced Migration and Mobility
  4. Prevention of Violent Extremism
  5. Environmental and Social Safeguarding
  6. Good Governance and Accountability

GOVERNANCE & LEADERSHIP

Strong governance. Responsible leadership.

VIRD's governance architecture supports accountability, strategic oversight and responsible programme implementation.

Governance structure

Board of Directors → Executive Leadership → Programme Management, Finance & Administration, MEAL, Safeguarding & Compliance, and Advisory Council → Community & Programme Structures.

Advisory Council

The Advisory Council provides strategic, technical and professional advice. Its potential expertise includes development, humanitarian response, Islamic finance, health, education, agriculture, WASH, finance, law, governance, research, technology, private sector and community development.

Appointments

Board and Advisory Council members will be published once formally confirmed. VIRD will not present unconfirmed individuals as office-holders.
